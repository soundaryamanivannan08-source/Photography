Got Questions?

FAQ Section

How do I book a session?
Booking is simple! Just head over to our Contact page, fill out the inquiry form, and we will get back to you within 24 hours to schedule a quick consultation.
How long is the delivery time?
For standard portrait sessions, delivery takes around 1-2 weeks. For weddings and large events, you can expect your fully edited gallery within 4-6 weeks.
Do you travel for shoots?
Absolutely! We love traveling for destination weddings and commercial shoots. Travel fees are calculated based on the destination and will be included in your custom quote.
What equipment do you use?
We shoot exclusively with high-end full-frame cameras (Canon EOS R5) paired with premium prime lenses to ensure the highest resolution and stunning low-light performance.
Can I customize a package?
Yes! While our standard packages cover most needs, we are more than happy to build a custom package tailored specifically to your event's unique requirements.

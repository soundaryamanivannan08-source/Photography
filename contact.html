Good to Know

Booking FAQ

How far in advance should I book?
For weddings, I recommend booking 9 to 12 months in advance as prime dates fill up quickly. For portraits and commercial shoots, 3 to 4 weeks is usually sufficient.
Do you require a deposit?
Yes, a 30% non-refundable retainer is required to secure your date, along with a signed digital contract. The remaining balance is due one week prior to the session.
Will we get the raw, unedited files?
I do not provide RAW files. Half of the magic happens in the editing room, and delivering RAW files is like a baker giving you a cake that isn't baked yet. You will receive fully edited, high-resolution JPEGs.
Do you travel for shoots?
Yes! I frequently travel domestically and internationally for elopements and commercial work. Travel fees are calculated transparently and added to your custom quote.
